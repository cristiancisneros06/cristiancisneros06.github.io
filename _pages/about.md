I'm an undergraduate at the University of Houston completing a B.S. with a double major in Physics and Mathematics (expected May 2027). I do research in nonlinear dynamics with Dr. Gemunu Gunaratne.

**I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.**

Research
======
My interests are **nonlinear dynamics**, **multifractals**, and **classification problems with convolutional neural networks (CNNs)**.

Many physical systems and processes can be reduced to, and analyzed as, discrete-time dynamical systems. I'm interested in their invariants: how a seemingly static picture evolves as the system's parameters vary, and how that change shows up in its dynamical properties.

On the machine-learning side, I study how measure-preserving data augmentation changes the signal a CNN learns and responds to in classification problems, currently for the Hénon map.
