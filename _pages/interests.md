**October 12, 2026**

Replaced the Ryzen 7 5700X with a Ryzen 9 5900XT, added an AIO liquid cooler, and installed a 1000 W power supply.

I went with the 5900XT because it's the best AM4 CPU readily available at my local parts retailer. A Ryzen 7 5800X3D would have been better for gaming, but for my research it made more sense to get the best CPU for my workload and the data analysis that comes with it. I also bought a better PSU: if I'm going to upgrade my GPU down the line, the PSU needs to be able to support it.

I'm cleaning up the cable management as I write this, but wanted to document the computer as it came along.
