Sequence logos summarize the nucleotides around sites such as exon–intron boundaries and branch points, but they assume each site is independent, so they hide correlations between neighboring sites. This paper maps sequence neighborhoods to points in the unit square, so that density plots of these point-sets reveal those higher-order correlations. We apply this to exon–intron boundaries, branch points, and normal versus cancer fusion junctions.

**My contribution:** I showed that point-sets built from sites within exons and within introns are multifractal, and that their singularity spectra f(α) distinguish the two, with intron sequences containing more high-density regions.
